About Me

Hi, I’m Reza Mohammadvand, a Computer Engineering graduate with a Master’s degree in Computer Networks from Iran University of Science and Technology. My interest in networks and IT infrastructure began during my undergraduate studies and grew to include cloud computing, edge computing, and the intersection of networking and artificial intelligence. My master’s thesis focused on edge intelligence.

My technical work has centered on designing, implementing, and optimizing network infrastructure, virtualization environments, and cloud platforms. I approach each system by considering its practical requirements alongside security, reliability, scalability, and efficient resource management.

I’m particularly interested in processing data close to where it is generated, where edge computing and AI can help reduce latency and support faster responses. My background in programming and web development also helps me understand how infrastructure decisions affect software and the people who use it.

I view distributed systems across multiple layers, from networks and protocols to software. Beyond my day-to-day work, I continue to explore the Internet of Things, 5G and 6G communications, cloud–edge convergence, and digital twins. I believe that continuous learning, research, and hands-on experience are essential to building systems people can rely on.
