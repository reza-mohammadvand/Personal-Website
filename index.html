About Me

Hi! I’m Reza Mohammadvand, a graduate with a Master’s degree in Computer Engineering, specializing in Computer Networks, from Iran University of Science and Technology. My interest in networking and IT infrastructure began during my undergraduate studies and gradually evolved as I delved into areas such as cloud computing and edge computing, shaping my professional path in this field.

In recent years, I have focused on designing, implementing, and optimizing network infrastructures at various scales. From building cloud computing platforms and virtualization environments to designing resilient, secure, and scalable architectures for modern applications, I’ve always strived to analyze technical needs thoroughly and deliver effective solutions.

My experience with edge computing architectures—especially those involving data processing close to the source—has allowed me to contribute meaningfully to systems requiring low latency and real-time responsiveness.

My skills in network structure analysis, infrastructure design, familiarity with communication security concepts, and proficiency in network monitoring and resource management tools have enabled me to play an active role in various projects. Alongside my networking expertise, I also have solid experience in web development and programming, which I’ve applied in both experimental and practical projects. My background in software development has helped me align infrastructure solutions with software needs and user experience considerations.

I hold a multidimensional perspective on distributed systems—from the network and protocol level to higher software layers—which has given me a deep understanding of the challenges in converging networking and cloud computing in scalable and real-time environments. From a research perspective, I have a sustained interest in areas such as the Internet of Things (IoT), 5G and 6G communications, Cloud-Edge convergence, and Digital Twins, and I continuously study these topics.

I believe that the combination of theoretical knowledge, hands-on experience, and a commitment to quality forms the foundation of professional service in the field of information technology. I always strive to uphold these principles in every aspect of my work.
